      Frequently asked questions

      • Can I try Tasklane before paying?

        Yes. Every account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is needed.

      • Can I change my plan later?

        You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply from your next billing date.

      • How is my data protected?

        All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily, and access is controlled by roles.

      • Does Tasklane work with Slack and GitHub?

        Yes. Connect them from the Integrations page to get task updates in Slack and link commits to tasks.

      • Do you offer discounts for yearly billing?

        Paying yearly saves 20% on Pro and Enterprise plans.
